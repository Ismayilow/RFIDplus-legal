Delete your RFIDplus account

Before publishing: replace every text in square brackets, then delete this box.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open RFIDplus and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Press Delete my account, then confirm.

Your account and all your data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app, send an email to ali.ismayilow@gmail.com from the email address you registered with. Use the subject “Delete my account”. We will confirm and delete your account within 3 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and sign-in details).
  • All assets, locations, and inventory checks stored in the cloud under your account.

Backups kept by our hosting provider may take up to 15 days to expire. We do not keep other copies of your data. Deleting cannot be undone.
