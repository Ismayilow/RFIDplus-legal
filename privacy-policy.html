Who we are

RFIDplus (“the app”) is provided by Ali Ismayilov, personal developer. You can contact us at ali.ismayilow@gmail.com.

What the app does

The app helps businesses keep track of their assets and inventory with RFID tags. You can register assets, scan tags with a compatible reader, run inventory checks, and share reports.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are stored in a protected (hashed) form. We cannot see your password.
  • Asset and inventory data you enter or import: asset names, categories, locations, RFID tag IDs, and the results and dates of your inventory checks.
  • Technical data: when the app syncs, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs such as your IP address and the time of the request.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or advertising ID. The app shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics tracking.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Internet: to sync your data and sign you in.
  • Bluetooth / nearby devices: only to connect to RFID readers that you choose to use. It is not used to track you.

How we use your information

We use it to run the app: to sign you in, to store and sync your assets and inventory checks across your devices, to keep your account secure, and to answer your support requests. We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising.

Where your data is stored and who processes it

Your account and synced data are stored in the cloud with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, on servers in East US (North Virginia). Supabase processes the data on our behalf. The app is distributed through Google Play. Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Some data is also stored on your own device so the app can work offline; it is removed when you sign out, delete your account, or uninstall the app.

How long we keep it, and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and all its data at any time:

  • On the web: our account deletion page, or email ali.ismayilow@gmail.com.

When you delete your account, your account information, assets, and inventory checks are removed from our systems. Backups kept by our hosting provider may take up to 15 days to expire.

Your rights

You can see and correct your data in the app, export it as Excel or PDF, and ask us to delete it. If you live in a region with data protection laws, you may have further rights, such as access, correction, or objection. Contact us and we will help.

Children

The app is made for businesses and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app or by email.

Contact

Questions about privacy: ali.ismayilow@gmail.com.
